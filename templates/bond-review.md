# Bond and labelled-debt review sheet

Identify issuer, guarantor, legal entity, currency, seniority, collateral, covenants, coupon, maturity, embedded options and recourse. Record documents and dates rather than infer terms from a marketing label.

## Credit case
Business/financial risk; climate cash-flow bridge; leverage/coverage; liquidity/refinancing; collateral and recovery; rating versus spread materiality. Separate risk-free, credit and liquidity drivers. Cite model assumptions and exclude double-counted expected-loss adjustments.

## Label/impact case — separate from credit
Use-of-proceeds: eligibility, selection, management of funds, allocation/refinancing, impact baseline, reports and external review.

Sustainability-linked: material KPI, boundary, baseline, target calibration, test/verification date, financial consequence, call interactions and incentive strength.

Transition: pathway consistency, project contribution, lock-in and offset/technology dependencies. Report current taxonomy/certification claims only with dated primary criteria and review.

## Final packet
Two separate conclusions: credit/valuation evidence and label/climate-contribution evidence. Include missing evidence, monitoring obligations, contrary case and human review status. Neither a green label nor avoided emissions establishes low credit risk.
