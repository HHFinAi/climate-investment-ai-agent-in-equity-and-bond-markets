# Three research casebooks and explicit falsifiers
**Research cutoff: 18 November 2026.** These are watchlists and mechanism tests, not issuer recommendations. The JSON numbers are *synthetic*; company-specific financial materiality, earnings and valuation remain **NEEDS_DATA**.

## 1. HVAC — TT, JCI, CARR
**Thesis to test:** more intense or frequent regional heat plus funded cooling retrofits generates incremental equipment and aftermarket free cash flow.
- Establish served geographies, installed base, order backlog, service recurring mix, installer capacity and electricity-price constraints.
- Distinguish weather adaptation from AI facilities, general construction and regulation-driven equipment replacement; do not sum them.
- Obtain issuer primary filings and regional capex authorization. Run 40/60/80% funding-to-revenue conversion with contribution-margin and working-capital stresses.
- **Falsifier:** order book increases without cash conversion, funded adoption or pricing power. Test security returns only once actual entry-price expectations are obtained.

## 2. Resilient power — GEV, HUBB, FLNC
**Thesis to test:** funded transmission/distribution hardening, grid equipment and selected storage create profitable orders.
- Map regulated utility budgets, network hazards, project permits, contract delivery years, supplier exposure and warranty obligations.
- Keep AI data-center load, conventional electrification and physical-risk resilience as overlapping co-drivers. Prevent double counting.
- Stress capex intensity, delay, customer cancellations, backlog receivables and augmentation.
- **Falsifier:** high booked backlog fails to translate into positive incremental free cash flow.

## 3. Flood defenses — Heidelberg Materials (HEI.DE)
**Thesis to test:** local concrete/cement requirements for awarded flood-protection projects lift margins where production and transport allow profitable capture.
- Verify actual infrastructure tenders, Heidelberg's relevant geographic assets, competitive supply, delivered freight cost and realized mix.
- Do NOT apply an entire aggregate flood protection TAM to one producer.
- Keep this **physical adaptation demand** case separate from HHFinAI's **Brevik CCS transition/mitigation** economics; no automatic valuation addition.
- **Falsifier:** projects are unfunded, cement intensity falls, or market share/logistics prevents price capture.

## Required common financial bridge
Location and hazard evidence → payer and committed budget → supplier awarded contract or operator avoided loss → issuer cash flow (revenue, pricing, costs, capex, working capital, insurance) → current security entry price and reverse valuation. Freeze scenario vintages and materially missing data. Explicitly keep one physical-risk ID in a single valuation channel to prevent charging it to both FCF and discount rate. For prospective El Niño research, freeze before NOAA/issuer outcomes; do not turn ENSO probabilities into portfolio weights or expected stock returns.

Primary-source workflow must obtain filings, local budgets, asset maps and dated market inputs. None of those company-specific inputs was verified for these synthetic benchmarks.
