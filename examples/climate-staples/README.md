# Climate and staples: the evidence needed between weather and earnings

**21 October 2026 · Research handoff, not a completed climate attribution model.**

Start with the independently developed [financial-materiality case](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/tree/main/examples/climate-staples): public H1 2026 disclosures for Nestlé, Unilever and Danone; a monthly input-cost model; original chart; source register; sensitivities; and contrary evidence. The model lives in one repository to avoid divergent copies.

The principal finding is methodological and financial: differently defined COGS can distort a common commodity-exposure assumption, while hedge, inventory and pricing clocks alter timing. This does not identify which issuer is most exposed to ENSO or assign a weather-driven earnings estimate. The background report guided the question, not the inputs or conclusion.

| Link in the causal argument | Evidence required | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| ENSO to local hazard | Dated seasonal outlook; crop region, season and hazard specificity; forecast uncertainty. | NOAA October 2026 outlook registered as context. Local probabilistic linkage remains NEEDS_DATA. |
| Hazard to crop supply | Agronomic sensitivity, soil/water conditions, harvest timing and counterfactual yield. | NEEDS_DATA. An ENSO label is not a crop-loss percentage. |
| Supply to market price | Stocks, substitutes, trade, demand, non-weather supply factors and timing. | NEEDS_DATA. No automatic weather-to-price coefficient. |
| Price to purchased inputs | Issuer commodity weights, contract currency, procurement geography, hedge basis/expiry and inventories. | NEEDS_DATA. Explicit common scenarios only. |
| Purchased costs to earnings | Pricing, elasticity, mix, volume, other costs and consistent accounting perimeter. | Public starting cost/margin structures; hypothetical lags and response model. |
| Earnings to equity or credit | Market expectations, tax/capital response, valuation; for bonds, leverage, liquidity, maturity and recourse. | NEEDS_DATA. No price target or spread call. |

The memo's Danone presentation control challenges an apparent issuer difference before interpreting it as climate risk. Review of an accounting issue does not validate the weather-to-procurement chain. A contrary case includes effective hedges, favorable currency, crop substitution and price recovery; demand weakness or basis mismatch can amplify costs.

Next acquire crop-region procurement evidence and validate the local hazard/supply mechanism before replacing scenario assumptions. Keep weather uncertainty, market uncertainty and issuer execution uncertainty separate. These standalone examples do not submit a research stage, receive human review or alter engine approval state.
