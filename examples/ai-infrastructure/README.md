# AI infrastructure: delivery economics, climate risk and financing boundaries

**Prepared 16 October 2026 | Primary-source context and illustrative scenarios | Investment conclusion: NEEDS_DATA.**

The [companion financial case](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/tree/main/examples/ai-infrastructure) asks when earlier capacity justifies additional power infrastructure. It contains original calculations, a dated project/policy register and public electricity-data analysis. Its hypothetical grid/gas comparison is not an issuer project valuation or a recommendation to finance fossil generation.

## Compare mechanisms on consistent boundaries

| Mechanism | Required evidence | Financial line or decision |
|---|---|---|
| Grid connection or dedicated power | Delivered schedule, capital contributions, energy/demand charges, reliability, fuel and standby terms | Capex, operating costs, commissioning and cancellation exposure |
| Cooling and physical resilience | Local hazard, design, water service, measured trade-offs and workload recovery | Incremental capex/opex and supported disruption severity |
| Emissions and transition | Ownership, operational boundary, activity, actual emissions factors and applicable obligations | Supported compliance costs and transition commitments |
| Operational disruption | Liability, service credits, recovery objectives and non-sensitive test evidence | Conditional cash-flow loss; no assumed occurrence probability |
| Financing and recourse | Borrower, guarantees, collateral, lease obligations, maturity and refinancing | Equity ownership bridge or instrument-specific credit analysis |

Water resilience is a physical-risk mechanism. Generation emissions and carbon obligations are transition mechanisms. Delay can arise from several causes; do not label every delivery problem climate risk or count the same disruption twice. Technical efficiency, climate impact and financial return require separate conclusions.

## Evidence and scenario discipline

The [source register](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/blob/main/examples/ai-infrastructure/evidence/sources.json) links Microsoft disclosures and actual Texas/New York policy texts. Policy scope and release conditions are not interchangeable, and the case has not verified their applicability to a named project. The [public-data analysis](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/blob/main/examples/ai-infrastructure/public-data/RESULTS.md) keeps EIA regional prices separate from utility contracts and LBNL generation/storage queues separate from data-center load connections.

The model's gas emissions factor and carbon costs are explicit assumptions, not a verified emissions inventory, enacted tax or regulatory forecast. It does not calculate lifecycle emissions, hourly grid matching, avoided emissions or an offset-adjusted footprint. No climate benefit is capitalized through an extra discount-rate adjustment.

## Equity and credit require different next steps

For **equity**, reconcile project ownership, incremental demand, financing and leases to a whole-company valuation and current market expectations. The companion per-share table only supplies scale using a dated share denominator.

For **credit**, identify the actual obligor and instrument before analyzing debt service, covenant headroom, maturity, refinancing and recovery. Project NPV is neither debt-service coverage nor a bond-price estimate. No debt structure or spread has been modeled here; a green or transition label would not substitute for those inputs.
