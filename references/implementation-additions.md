# Source-derived method versus implementation additions

The methodological backbone—physical and transition risks; mitigation, adaptation and resilience; evidence limitations; scenario analysis; financial transmission; differentiated equity and fixed-income analysis; stewardship, portfolio management and mandate accountability—comes from the mapped CFA UK chapters.

The following are this project's original engineering choices: twenty roles, seven DAG routes, IDs and artifact schemas, frozen inputs, SHA-256 provenance, stale-write protection, missing-data stops, severity categories, explicit review attestation, a provider-neutral host protocol, Python helpers, synthetic fixtures, validation tests, release manifest, CI and guarded private publishing. The source does not prescribe this agent architecture.

The source's educational examples and current-at-the-time regulations were not copied into operational defaults. No present-day legal or climate-data refresh was undertaken. In a real research run, the host should add current primary evidence and record any change in assumptions or framework explicitly rather than silently editing history.

The source's normative climate objectives are preserved as concepts but not imposed as the investor's mandate. Client objectives must be explicit. Political analysis is confined to neutral documented facts and financial sensitivities. Source material about engagement does not grant permission for external communication or advocacy.
